# Book Cover Prompts — *Designing Android at Scale* (5 volumes)

Ready-to-paste prompts for **ChatGPT (GPT-4o / DALL·E image generation)** to create a **cohesive,
premium, 5-volume series** of covers. Each cover shares one design language and changes only the
**accent color**, **volume number**, and **subtitle**, so the five sit together as a set.

**Target spec:** 1600 × 2560 px (portrait, 1.6:1), RGB — the KDP Kindle eBook cover size.

> **Important about text on AI covers:** image models often render small text imperfectly. Two
> good workflows:
> 1. **Recommended:** ask ChatGPT for the **artwork/background only** (first prompt below), then
>    add the title text yourself in Canva / Figma / Photoshop using the exact text in each
>    volume's "Text to place" block — crisp, correct, and easy to keep consistent.
> 2. **One-shot:** use the full per-volume prompt (which includes the text) and accept that you
>    may need to regenerate a few times or touch up the lettering.
>
> Generate all five in **one chat session** and tell ChatGPT to "keep the composition, layout,
> fonts, and style identical to the previous image; change only the accent color, volume number,
> and subtitle" so the set stays consistent.

---

## 0. Shared art-direction brief (paste first, once)

```
You are designing a cohesive 5-volume series of covers for a premium technical book for senior
software engineers, titled "Designing Android at Scale". I will ask for each volume one at a
time. Keep ONE consistent design system across all five; only the accent color, the large volume
number, and the subtitle change between volumes.

Design system (keep identical across all 5):
- Portrait book cover, 1600 x 2560 px, 1.6:1 ratio, RGB, high resolution, print-quality.
- Mood: modern, architectural, confident, minimal, "systems design" — NOT cartoonish, NO mascots,
  NO real company logos or brand marks of any kind.
- Background: deep near-black charcoal (#0E1116) with a very subtle darker-to-lighter vertical
  gradient and faint fine grid lines, like an engineering blueprint.
- Central motif: an elegant, abstract "system architecture" diagram made of thin glowing lines
  connecting layered nodes, rounded rectangles, and small circular endpoints — suggesting app
  layers, data flow, and modules. It should feel like a clean architecture diagram rendered as
  art: geometric, precise, softly glowing in the accent color, with subtle depth and a faint
  bloom. Low visual noise, lots of negative space, premium and uncluttered.
- Typography zones (leave clean space for these): a slim top band for the small series title, a
  large lower-third area for the volume subtitle, a bold oversized volume number as a design
  element, and a bottom line for the author.
- Accent color is used only for the glowing diagram lines, the volume number, and thin accent
  rules. Everything else is charcoal, off-white (#E8EAED), and muted grey.
- Style references: the restraint of modern enterprise-software branding and editorial science
  magazine covers; clean, geometric, luminous line-art. Flat-ish with subtle glow and depth. No
  photorealism, no 3D clutter, no stock-photo people.

Confirm you understand, then I'll give you Volume 1.
```

## Tips

- Generate at the largest size the tool allows, then scale/crop to exactly **1600 × 2560**.
- If text comes out garbled, ask for **"the same cover with no text, just the artwork and empty
  text zones"** and typeset the titles yourself for crisp results.
- Keep the author name identical on all five.
- Do **not** include the Android robot, Google/Amazon/Kindle logos, or any real brand mark — keep
  the art original and generic to avoid trademark issues at review.
- For paperback spines/back covers later, reuse the same accent + background and the KDP Print
  Cover Calculator dimensions.
